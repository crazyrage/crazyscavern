>> START HERE

SourceCraft is an RPG mod: you start on a random free race (switch any time with !changerace), play the campaign like normal, earn XP to level up your race's skills, and spend crystals on items. Progress saves to your Steam account. When in doubt, type !menu in chat.

>> GAME MODES

Every server takes Campaign, Realism and Versus lobbies, and these mutations: Last Man on Earth, Headshot!, Hard Eight, Four Swordsmen, Chainsaw Massacre, Last Gnome on Earth, Room for One, Gib Fest, Hunting Party, Lone Gunman, Healing Gnome, Special Delivery, Flu Season, Death's Door. Pick the mode in your lobby as usual; your race and levels come with you. Any other mutation is played as plain Campaign or Versus.

Every server is ready for 5 to 8 players. A campaign seats up to 8 survivors, versus up to 8 a side. A lobby still holds 4 players (8 in versus), so once those seats are full more players join the running game: from Steam Group Servers (join the group crazyscavern), a friend's Join Game, or connect with an address above. The server says in chat when the extra seats open. In a campaign each extra player gets a new survivor. In versus an extra player types !join to play a survivor, or picks the infected in the team menu.

More survivors, tougher infected. The hordes, the Witch and the Tank grow with the survivors on the team, bots included: x1.25 at 5, x1.5 at 6, x1.65 at 7 and x1.8 at 8. It changes as players join and leave.

The Advanced servers start with 6 survivors (bots fill the empty seats); the Normal servers start with 4. Your lobby can still pick the map, mode and difficulty on any server. Last Man on Earth and Lone Gunman are one-player modes.

!prestigePrestige at overall level 64; !prestige top for the leaderboard

Each level gives a skill point. The skill menu opens on its own at calm moments (in saferooms, when you respawn or die); !spendskills opens it any time.

Abilities need a key. Open the console (~) and type e.g. bind f +ultimate1 (up to +ultimate4 for races with more abilities). Menus show on the left of the screen; pick with the number keys.

>> RACES

Free: Human Alliance, Night Elf, Orcish Horde, Undead.
Unlocked by overall level (all your races' levels added up): Zealot, Marine, UAW (16) - Ghost (32) - Templar, Reaper (48) - Dark Templar, Phoenix (64).

Some races evolve with an ultimate into stronger forms (e.g. Zealot into Dragoon, then Reaver). Dying drops you back one step. Races that heal themselves over time aren't available to survivors.

Infected play melee races only: Zergling and Zealot (overall level 16), Omegalisk (32), Dark Templar (64). Before level 16 you get Zergling or Zealot to play anyway; as a survivor you go back to a race you've unlocked.

Changing race is instant until someone leaves the saferoom; after that it applies when you next spawn.

>> EARNING XP

Just play well as a team. You get XP for killing commons and specials (helping kill one counts as an assist), helping kill the Witch or the Tank, reviving, healing or defibbing teammates, reaching the saferoom and surviving the finale. Higher difficulty pays more: Advanced +25%, Expert +50%.

Common kills add up and are paid in one chat line every 10 seconds. Kills also give crystals for the shop.

Prestige: at overall level 64, type !prestige. Your race levels start over from 0, but your races stay unlocked and you keep your items and crystals, plus a permanent XP bonus that grows with each prestige (up to the 5th). !prestige top shows the leaderboard.

>> COMMON INFECTED

Every common spawns with a SourceCraft race and a tier. Commons only get passive bonuses, never abilities:

RACEWHAT TO EXPECT
ZealotLight: hits a bit harder, small shield
Dark TemplarLight, but partly invisible: watch for shimmering commons
Zergling, OmegaliskMedium: armor and health regeneration
Ultralisk, Dark ArchonHeavy: hit hard and carry thick armor that soaks part of every hit

The tier (1-4) says how many of those bonuses are switched on: tier 1 has one, tier 4 has everything at full strength. Harder difficulties spawn more heavies and higher tiers, so on Expert expect a lot of tanky commons. Tip: focus fire on the big ones and don't let regenerating ones walk off.

Your race's on-hit skills and your items work on commons and the Witch too: bonus damage, fire, plague, lifesteal, Claws of Attack, and Thorns or Feedback when they hit you. Stuns and slows don't (commons can't be slowed).

Mask of Death heals only on hits on specials, Tanks and the Witch, not on commons.

>> SPECIAL INFECTED

Specials (Hunter, Smoker, Charger, Jockey and the rest) also get a SourceCraft race: one of the melee races above, with every skill maxed. They keep their normal pounce, pull or charge and use their race's abilities and ultimates on top. Treat every special as a priority target.

Killing a special pays bonus XP, and anyone who damaged it gets an assist. For the Tank you need about 15% of its health for the assist; for the Witch, 10%.

In Versus (on any server) you play the specials yourself with one of these races.

>> SHOP

You start with 50 crystals. Type !shopmenu to spend them. Good picks to start: a Ring of Regeneration (slow healing over time) and the Ammo Pack (refills your gun's reserve ammo).

Your items last the whole campaign while you survive: reach the saferoom alive and you keep them on the next map. You lose them when you die, when your team wipes, when you leave, or when a new campaign starts. In versus, survivor and infected items are kept separately: what you buy on one side stays on that side for your next turn there.
