Getting started

  1. Join. Your first time, you start as one of the four classic races at random (Human Alliance, Orcish Horde, Night Elf or Undead Scourge; all four are open to everyone). Type !race to pick another (factions with several races open a second page). You also start with 50 crystals.
  2. Your first kill on a race takes it to level 1. After that, kills earn XP. Every race level gives one skill point. The skills menu opens by itself when you next respawn (or type !skills).
  3. At race level 8 you can learn an ultimate. Bind keys for them (below).
  4. Kills also pay crystals: type !shop to buy items for this life.

Menus appear in the middle of the screen: W/S to move, D to pick, A to go back, Tab to close. You can't move while a menu is open (you can still look around); shooting closes it, and so does getting shot. The line under the options explains the highlighted one (what a skill or item does, or why it's greyed out).

How it works

Respawn mode

You respawn right away with 2 seconds of spawn protection. You can buy CS2 weapons anywhere, at any time; everyone starts with $800. No bomb; each map lasts 30 minutes, then the next one in the rotation loads (chat warns you a minute before; your progress is saved). Teams auto-balance.

Bots

Each team has 5 bots (CS2's Expert difficulty), and they play SourceCraft too:

  • Every bot has a race, any race, including ones you haven't unlocked yet.
  • Their level changes every life, from a quarter of the race's max up to max, mostly on the low side: most bots are half-built, a few are fully built. Their skill points are already spent.
  • From race level 8 they use their ultimates (never the ones that transform them).
  • They buy one shop item each life, but carry no crystals, so there's nothing to loot from them.

XP and levels

  • Kills: XP by the victim's race level (6 XP at level 0 up to 100 at level 16), plus 1 per overall level they're above you. Headshot +10, knife kill +20, assist +10.
  • Bots are worth the same as a kill on a player at your own level.
  • XP and crystals count once at least 2 players are on the server; bots count, so you can level against bots alone.
  • Each race level needs more XP than the last: 100 for level 1, then 200, 400, 800, 1,600, 3,200, 6,400, 12,800 (level 8), 25,600, 51,200, then +10,000 more for each level after.
  • Skill points: one per race level. A new point takes effect at your next spawn. Leveling up shows a reminder; nothing pops up in the middle of a fight.
  • A race's max level is the total of its skills' max levels.
  • Overall level is all your race levels added together. It unlocks more races:

Skills and ultimates

  • Passive skills work on their own once you put points in them.
  • Ultimates are used with css_ultimate1 to css_ultimate4 (bind them). They need race level 8 unless the race list says otherwise.
  • One ultimate per slot: some races offer two ultimates on the same key; you can learn only one of them.
  • Cooldowns keep running while you're dead, so dying doesn't reset them.

Energy

  • You spawn with your race's starting energy (usually 30) and gain 1 per second, up to its max (usually 100).
  • Ultimates and some passives cost energy.
  • Banked energy: when you die, energy above your race's starting amount is banked for that race (up to 1,000; shown in brackets on the readout). Some big ultimates can draw from it.

Evolutions

Some ultimates turn you into another race until you die (Zealot into Dragoon, Marine into Firebat, and so on). An evolution has its own levels and skill points; when you die you are back to your normal race. Evolutions aren't in the race menu: you reach them through their parent race. There are 21 races to pick and 15 evolutions.

Crystals, vespene and the shop

  • Crystals: 2 per kill or assist (you can hold up to 100).
  • Vespene: 1 per kill (up to 5,000); a few items and skills cost it.
  • Items last until you die. An Ankh of Reincarnation brings them back once. Items aren't kept when you leave the server; crystals and vespene are.

Differences from TF2 SourceCraft

  • No classes: every race works with any CS2 weapon.
  • Everyone has 100 HP, so flat skill damage from TF2 (Chain Lightning, Suicide Bomber...) is scaled down.
  • Some skills were rebuilt for CS2: Firebat is built around molotovs, Vulture throws real grenades, Reaver scarabs and mines are custom.
  • Not ported: the building races (Protoss Probe, Phase Prism, Carrier; Zerg Drone, Hive Queen) and Zerg Lurker. A few skills that needed TF2 (Uber charges, boss summons, mind control...) were cut.

  • Adrenal Glands (max 4, 2 energy per use): Knife hits do 15-65% more damage.

evolution of Zerg Queen, until you die; max level 8. A Queen's victim for 10s: no weapons, fast, and explodes (hurting everyone near).
