>> START HERE

We run two servers. Vanilla+ (30 slots) is the plain game with a few extras. SourceCraft (25 slots) adds races, levels, skills and a shop. Find both in the game's server list: search Crazy's Cavern.

There is no text chat in SCP:SL, so everything here happens in two places: the console (press ~; our commands start with a dot, like .servers) and the settings (Esc > Settings > Server-specific).

>> SOURCECRAFT

Still in testing: XP and crystals are x7 for now and count once 2 players are on. Levels may be wiped once balancing and tuning are done. Feedback is welcome on our Discord.

You get a race, earn XP to level it, put skill points into its skills and spend crystals in the shop. Humans and SCPs have separate races: you keep one of each.

The menu: Esc > Settings > Server-specific > SourceCraft, with the pages Me, Races, Skills, Shop and Help. Set your ultimate keys at the bottom of that page: no key is set until you pick one. While alive, your energy and crystals show at the bottom left.

CONSOLE (~)WHAT IT DOES
.scYour race, level, XP, energy and crystals, and this list
.raceThe races with their unlock levels; .race <name> picks one
.skillsYour race's skills; .skill 1 4 puts four points into skill 1, .resetskills takes them all back
.ult 1 ... .ult 4Use an ultimate (the keys in the menu do the same)
.shop / .buyThe shop; .buy boots
.sctopThe ten highest overall levels

Which race you play

YOU AREYOU PLAY
Class-D, scientist, guard, MTF, ChaosYour human race (a random starter race until you pick one)
An SCPYour SCP race: Zergling or Zealot to begin with, even before level 16
A zombieAlways Zergling, and only a zombie can morph into a Baneling
SCP-079Any race you've unlocked; no skills, it only earns XP for that race

Changing race: one change a round counts, in its first 60 seconds. A later change takes effect next round. Between rounds, change as often as you like. Alive, you pick for the side you're on; dead or in the lobby, for either side.

XP, levels and crystals

  • XP for kills (20 to 63 by the victim's race level, more if they're above your overall level), killing an SCP (kill XP x5), damage you take off an SCP, escaping (100), and being alive on the winning side at round end (50).
  • Your first kill on a new race takes it to level 1. Each level gives a skill point. Ultimates unlock at race level 8.
  • Overall level is all your race levels added up; it unlocks more races.
  • Crystals: 50 to start, 2 per kill, 10 per SCP kill, 5 per escape, at most 100.
  • Energy pays for skills. Energy left when you die is banked for the big ultimates.
  • Escaping, or a new role from staff, keeps your items and energy; only a death or a new round ends them.
  • The game's instant kills stay as they are; only Night Elf's Evasion can dodge one.

The menu's Skills page and .skills show every skill's numbers. Against an SCP that kills outright, damage skills do nothing; speed, armor, shields, regeneration, cloaking and the ultimates work on every SCP.

The shop

Items last one life (an Ankh brings them back once). Keycards, guns and SCP items are never for sale. An SCP can buy the mod's items but no game items; SCP-079 can't buy.

Do Not Track

If you have Do Not Track on in the game, your SourceCraft progress lasts for that visit only. Type .sc optin to have it kept, .sc optout to have it deleted.
