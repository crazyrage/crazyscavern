Left 4 Dead 2

>> NEW HERE?

  • Join any server. You start on a race; type !menu for races, skills and the shop.
  • Play the game as usual: XP comes from playing, and every level gives a skill point.
  • Higher levels unlock more races. Each game's guide has the details.
  • Join the Steam group to find us again. In L4D2, group members see every server under Steam Group Servers.
