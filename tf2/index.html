>> START HERE

Crazy's Cavern runs SourceCraft - a classic TF2 RPG mod, restored and maintained by CrazyRage. Pick a race, earn XP by playing TF2 like normal, level skills, buy items with crystals. Progress saves to your Steam account. When in doubt: type !menu in chat.

>> COMMANDS

!racePick or switch your race (applies on respawn); !race orc picks one by name

Ultimate keybind: bind mouse3 +ultimate in console. A race with more than one ultimate shows a number next to each in the skill menu (Ultimate 2, Ultimate 5...): bind those with the number, e.g. bind mouse4 +ultimate2. Item actives: +item1 ... +item9.

>> PROGRESSION

XP from kills/objectives → levels → skill points. Crystals buy shop items. Vespene is the rare resource. Energy is your mana. Overall level (everything combined) unlocks higher races. Summoned races (Dragoon, Necromancer, Battlecruiser... anything you become until you die) earn 3x XP (24x on TRADE, 15x on NA 5X).

Loot packages: kills sometimes drop a loot box with bonus XP/crystals. It's pure profit - the fallen player loses nothing - and whoever touches it first keeps it. Race your team to the box.

>> MAIN RACES (new!)

Every player gets 3 MAIN TOKENS per season. Playing a race you love? Type !main to commit to it: the race is announced to the server and rolls a permanent x2.5 to x4.0 XP bonus - the roll is luck, choose wisely, it's locked for the season.

And yes: there is a 1-in-1,000,000 chance the moment you main a race that it INSTANTLY MAXES OUT. Everyone will hear about it. Good luck.

Check your mains anytime with !mains. Whispers speak of a sealed Reroll Shrine...

>> BOSSES & EVENTS

The Necromancer raises the dead to fight at his side, and the admin runs Monoculus / Horsemann boss events on event maps. If the ground shakes, that's not a bug.

MvM co-op server: bring your races into Mann-vs-Machine at 148.113.183.190:27016 - robots pay full XP there, solo-friendly, separate progression from the main servers. Advanced/Expert missions.

TRADE sandbox server: 148.113.183.190:27017 - trade maps (Plaza, Minecraft Neon, Liquid Urbs, Rawr) with a map vote, 15x XP, instant respawn and 4 bots that stand at spawn. It keeps its own levels, separate from the main servers: level up fast, try races you haven't reached on main yet, mess around with skills, and trade while you're at it. Nothing you do there touches your main progress.

NA 5X server: 148.113.183.190:27032 - the normal maps with 10 bots and 5x XP (summoned races 15x). Like TRADE it keeps its own levels, separate from the main servers.
